# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project. Participation is open regardless
of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and safety boundaries.
- Make room for contributors with different levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and
repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, discussions, issues, and project-related
public spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a
participant when behavior puts people or the project at risk.

For an urgent safety concern, contact the repository maintainer privately through their GitHub
profile. If no private channel is available, open an issue requesting private contact without
including sensitive details. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.
